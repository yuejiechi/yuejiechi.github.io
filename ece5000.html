ECE 5000 Introduction to Analog and Digital Communications

Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, The Ohio State University

Fall 2012

Instructor: Dr. Yuejie Chi

Syllabus

Lecture Outline (Carmen)

  • introduction
  • review of signal and systems
  • analog modulation
  • review of probability
  • review of random processes
  • Nyquist sampling theorem
  • complex baseband representation
  • digital communications, transmitter
  • digital communications, receiver
  • digital realizations of transmitter and receivers
  • detection and symbol error rate analysis
  • equalization in flat fading channel
  • equalization in selective fading channel
  • single carrier frequency domain equalization
  • orthogonal frequency division multiplexing
  • frequency offset equalization
  • diversity techniques and multi-antenna communication
  • error correcting codes
  • Viterbi algorithm
